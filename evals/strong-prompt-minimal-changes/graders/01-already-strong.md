PASS if the reply recognizes that the prompt is already strong (or says so in other words) and either asks at most 2 questions, or presents an enhanced version that keeps the user's wording, structure and rules largely intact with small edits. FAIL if it asks 3 or more questions, or rewrites the prompt substantially, or drops any of the user's rules (two-sentence summary, at most 6 bullets in three groups, under 20 words, no ticket numbers, leave out internal changes, 'Needs review' list, friendly tone).
